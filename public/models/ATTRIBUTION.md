# Vehicle visualization asset

Ferrari 458 Italia model by vicent091036, distributed in the official Three.js car materials example.

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb

Original creator link provided by the example: https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6

Example and attribution: https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_materials_car.html

Local file: inspection-car.glb. Geometry is unchanged; the presentation applies graphite body paint and dark glass at runtime. This is illustrative vehicle imagery, not a Vtest-manufactured vehicle or a statement of manufacturer endorsement.

The source repository's license is retained alongside this file. The original creator listing is currently unavailable; a separate asset-specific license could not be verified from that listing.
